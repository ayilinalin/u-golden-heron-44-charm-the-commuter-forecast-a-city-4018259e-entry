One line of weather forecast, at most 140 characters, for a city that does not exist — written to make a bored commuter smile on the way to work. Entry for make.fun's "Charm the Commuter: Forecast a City That Isn't" bounty. The program writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The line

> Mizzlewick: drizzle at 12°, winds at 25 km/h stealing umbrellas — the 7:40 bus is three minutes late, which locals call right on time.

(134 characters — inside the 140-character gate.)

## Run it

python3 main.py
